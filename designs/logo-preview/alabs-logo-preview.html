aLabs

Fifteen directions for the aLabs mark

Three batches. 01–05 wrap an idea around the same geometric lowercase a. 06–10 leave the letter behind — pure geometry and one fused monogram. 11–15 reach for the execution cues a tech brand actually ships: negative space, a continuous line, a folded plane, a live signal, a module grid. Every concept is shown at the sizes that matter: from a 128 px hero down to a 16 px favicon, on light, dark, and pure monochrome.

Batch 02

Abstract & structural directions

Batch 01 (above) wraps ideas around the letter a. This batch leaves the letter behind — pure geometry and one fused monogram — for a mark that reads as a system, not a wordmark. Aperture (06) is the strongest here and a genuine alternative to Orbit (01).

06

Aperture

Recommended

No letter — three thick arc-blades offset 120° around a glowing accent core, reading as a camera iris or scientific aperture. It owns the “labs / precision instrument” idea more directly than any letterform can, and the rotational silhouette stays unmistakable down to 16 px. The strongest mark in this batch and a real alternative to Orbit (01).

Batch 03

Craft & motion directions

Batch 01 wrapped ideas around the letter a; Batch 02 went abstract. This batch reaches for the execution cues a tech brand actually ships — negative space, a single continuous line, a folded plane, a live signal, a module grid. Prisma (11) is the most premium mark in the whole set and the strongest app-icon.

11

Prisma

Recommended

Not a new letterform — a new technique. The a is knocked out of a solid rounded tile so the surface shows through the letter, with a single accent dot glowing in the counter. Negative-space marks read as the most premium tier of branding (Adobe, Beats, the Guild of Food Writers fork-tongue) and this is the strongest app-icon in the whole set — its solid mass holds weight at 16 px where every thin-stroke mark on this page starts to collapse.

12

Continuum

A single continuous stroke forming an infinity loop, knotted at an accent center — flow, feedback, always-on delivery. One unbroken line is the most elegant construction on the page: no fills, no joints to misalign, and it scales by stroke alone. It also never competes with the wordmark, which is why it pairs cleanest in the lockups.
